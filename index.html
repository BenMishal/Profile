💬 Ask the Analyst

My approach begins with strict feature engineering to ensure data quality. I then select algorithms (like Random Forest or XGBoost) based on trade-offs between interpretability and accuracy, followed by robust hyperparameter tuning and cross-validation to prevent overfitting.

I utilize frameworks like BPMN to visually document current-state ("as-is") workflows. By consulting cross-functional stakeholders, I identify redundancies and bottlenecks to propose a streamlined future-state ("to-be") model.

I prioritize data visualization via Power BI or Tableau to create intuitive dashboards. I avoid technical jargon and instead frame metrics purely around business impact—focusing on ROI, risk reduction, and strategic alignment.
